# Invitations

This page covers invitations from both sides: how to invite users to a Workspace, and how to accept an invitation you have received.

* * *

## Inviting users to your workspace​

Workspace Admins can invite users directly from **Workspace Settings**.

**To open it:** navigate to your workspace, click **Settings** in the left sidebar, then scroll to the **Users** section.

### Send an invitation​

  1. Click **\+ Invite** in the Users section.
  2. In the **Invite users** panel, enter one or more email addresses in the **User email(s)** field. You can select existing organization members from the dropdown or type a new email address.
  3. Click **Send invites**.

The system sends an invitation email to each address. The invited user appears in the member list with an **INVITING** status until they accept.

* * *

## Accepting an invitation​

The Invitations page shows all pending workspace invitations sent to your account.

**To open it:** click **Go to Organization** at the bottom of the left sidebar, then select **Invitations** in the Settings menu.

Column| Description| **Organization name**|  The organization that invited you| **Workspace name**|  The specific workspace within that organization| **Invited at**|  Date and time the invitation was sent| **Status**| **Accept** button if pending; **Access** link once accepted  
---|---  
  
Use the search box to filter by organization name.

**To accept:**

  1. Find the invitation row and click **Accept**.
  2. A "Invitation accepted successfully" toast appears.
  3. The row's status changes to **Access** , which links directly to the workspace.

note

Invitations expire after 24 hours. Only a Workspace Admin or Organization Owner can change your role after you join.
