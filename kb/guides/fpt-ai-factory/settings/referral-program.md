# Referral Program

The Referral Program lets you earn credits by inviting new users to FPT AI Factory. When someone signs up using your referral link and makes their first qualifying purchase, both you and the referred user receive credits applied to your Wallet.

* * *

## How it works​

  1. Go to **Settings → Referral Program** in your organization.
  2. Copy your unique referral link or code.
  3. Share it with colleagues, teammates, or anyone who might benefit from FPT AI Factory.
  4. When a referred user signs up and completes a qualifying action, credits are automatically added to both Wallets.
