# Billing Settings

The Billing Settings page lets you manage your account information, view your billing currency, and configure a low-balance alert for your Organization Wallet.

## Go to Billing Settings​

In the left sidebar, click **Billing** , then click **Billing settings**.

* * *

## Account information​

The **Account information** section stores the details used on invoices and receipts.

### View and edit account information​

  1. Click **Edit** to switch to edit mode.
  2. Update any of the following fields:

Field| Description| **Account type**| **Personal** or **Company**| **Name**|  Full name or company name| **Email**|  Billing contact email address| **Country**|  Country of residence or company registration| **Address**|  Billing address (required)| **Tax ID / National ID**|  Tax identification number (for companies) or national ID (for individuals)  
---|---  
  
  3. Click **Save** to apply changes, or **Cancel** to discard.

* * *

## Billing currency​

The **Billing currency** section displays the currency currently set for your Organization. This value is locked and cannot be changed after your first top-up.

Supported currencies: **USD** , **VND** , **JPY**.

warning

Choose your currency carefully during the first top-up. Once set, it applies to all future transactions and cannot be switched.

* * *

## Low balance alert​

The low-balance alert sends an email notification when your Organization Wallet drops below a threshold you define, giving you time to top up before running resources are interrupted.

### Enable the alert​

  1. In the **Low balance alert** section, check **Send email by credit threshold**.
  2. Enter a **Credit threshold** in USD — for example, `50` to be notified when the balance falls below $50.
  3. Enter one or more **Recipient emails**. To add multiple addresses, separate them with commas.
  4. Click **Save**.

### Disable the alert​

  1. Uncheck **Send email by credit threshold**.
  2. Click **Save**.
