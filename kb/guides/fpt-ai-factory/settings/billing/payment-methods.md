# Payment Methods

The Payment Methods page lets you save and manage the cards used for topping up your Organization Wallet. Saved cards are available as a quick-select option during checkout.

## View your saved cards​

In the left sidebar, click **Billing**. The **Payments** section is on the Billing page.

Each saved card shows:

Column| What it shows| **Card**|  Card brand (Visa, Mastercard) and last four digits| **Nickname**|  A label you assigned to the card| **Expiry date**|  Month and year the card expires| **Default**|  Star icon — filled if this card is the default  
---|---  
  
* * *

## Add a payment method​

  1. In the **Payments** section, click **\+ Add card**.
  2. In the dialog, enter your card details: 
     * **Card number**
     * **Expiration date** (MM / YY)
     * **CVC**
     * **Country**
  3. Click **Save**.

The card is saved and appears in the list. If it is your first card, it is automatically set as the default.

* * *

## Set a card as default​

Click the star icon next to the card you want to set as default. The star fills to indicate the card is now the default. The default card is pre-selected at checkout.

* * *

## Edit a card nickname​

  1. Click the edit icon next to the card.
  2. In the dialog, update the **Nickname** field.
  3. Click **Save**.

* * *

## Delete a card​

  1. Click the delete icon next to the card.
  2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog.

note

You cannot delete the default card while it is the only saved card. Add another card first, set it as default, then delete the original.

* * *
