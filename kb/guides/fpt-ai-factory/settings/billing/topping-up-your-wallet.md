# Top Up

Top up your Organization Wallet to add credits for running GPU resources. Credits are deducted as Workspaces consume resources.

## 1\. Go to Billing​

In the left sidebar, click **Billing**. The Billing overview page opens, showing your current Organization balance.

## 2\. Click Pay now​

Click the **Pay now** button in the **Organization balance** section. The **Account information** dialog opens.

## 3\. Fill in account information​

  1. Select your account type: **Personal** or **Company**.
  2. Fill in all required fields: **Name** , **Email** , **Address** , **Country** , and **Tax ID or National ID**.
  3. Under **Billing currency** , select the currency for this purchase.

warning

The billing currency cannot be changed after your first purchase. If you select VND now, all future top-ups must also use VND.

  5. Click **Continue**.

## 4\. Choose an amount​

The Add credits dialog opens on **Step 1 of 2**.

  1. Select a preset amount or enter a custom amount. Preset values depend on your billing currency (e.g., 500,000 / 1,000,000 / 3,000,000 for VND; $25 / $50 / $100 for USD).
  2. Click **Continue**.

## 5\. Select a payment method​

The dialog moves to **Step 2 of 2**.

  1. Select a payment method: 
     * **VNPAY** — Visa, Mastercard, JCB, UnionPay; domestic ATM/bank accounts; VNPAY-QR; supports VAT and commercial invoices
     * **PayPal** — PayPal balance or linked cards
     * **Stripe** — international credit/debit cards
  2. Enter your payment details for the selected method.
  3. Click **Continue** to proceed to the payment gateway.

note

All top-up transactions are non-refundable once processed.

* * *

## After the top-up​

Your Wallet balance updates as soon as the payment is confirmed. The **Top-up** line in the Organization balance section reflects the new amount.

## Set up a low balance alert​

A low balance alert sends an email when your Organization Wallet drops below a threshold you set — so you can top up before GPU resources are interrupted.

  1. In the left sidebar, click **Billing** , then click **Billing settings**.
  2. In the **Low balance alert** section, check **Send me an email when my balance falls below the credit threshold.**
  3. Enter a **Credit threshold** in USD — for example, `100` to be notified when the balance drops below $100.
  4. Enter one or more **Recipient emails**. Separate multiple addresses with commas.
  5. Click **Save**.
