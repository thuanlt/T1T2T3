# Payment History

The Payments page shows a record of all top-up transactions for your Organization. You can download an invoice for any payment.

## 1\. Go to Payments​

In the left sidebar, click **Billing** , then click **Payments**.

## 2\. Find a payment​

The table shows the following columns for each transaction:

Column| What it shows| **Date**|  Date and time the payment was processed| **Source**|  Payment method used (e.g., VNPAY, Stripe, PayPal)| **Recipient email**|  Email address the invoice was sent to| **Amount**|  Amount paid| **Invoice**|  Download icon — click to download the invoice PDF  
---|---  
  
## 3\. Download an invoice​

Click the download icon in the **Invoice** column for the payment you want. The invoice PDF downloads to your browser.
