# Billing Plans

A Billing Plan is a prepaid credit allocation purchased through FPT that covers specific services over a fixed time period. Plans offer a cost-effective alternative to pay-as-you-go top-ups for teams with predictable usage.

## View your Billing Plans​

Click **Go to Organization** at the bottom of the left sidebar, then click **Billing** → **Billing plans** tab.

The page header shows four summary cards:

Card| What it shows| **Active plans**|  Number of currently active plans| **Expired plans**|  Number of plans that have ended| **Total spent**|  Total amount consumed across all plans, with percentage change vs. last month| **Total plan amount**|  Sum of all plan values and the remaining unused amount  
---|---  
  
* * *

## Filter and search plans​

Use the filter bar to narrow the list:

Filter| Options| **Search**|  Search by plan code or plan type| **Service**|  Filter by one or more services covered by the plan| **Status**|  Active, Expired, or Inactive| **Date range**|  Filter by plan start or end date  
---|---  
  
* * *

## Understand the plan list​

Each row in the table shows:

Column| What it shows| **Billing plans**|  Plan type and unique plan code| **Service**|  Services covered by the plan| **Duration**|  Start date, end date, and days remaining| **Status**|  Active, Expired, or Inactive| **Usage (USD)**|  Amount consumed and a progress bar| **Payment status**|  Paid, Unpaid, or Partially paid  
---|---  
  
If a plan has an **Unpaid** or **Partially paid** status, a **Pay now** button appears inline. Click it to complete the payment.

* * *

## View plan details​

Click any row in the list to open the plan detail page.

### Quick stats​

Three pills at the top of the page show:

  * Current **status** of the plan
  * **Days remaining** until the plan expires
  * **Usage percentage** consumed so far

### Plan information​

The detail page is divided into four sections:

**Usage overview**

  * Amount consumed vs. total plan value
  * Usage progress bar
  * Remaining balance
  * Estimated days until the plan balance is depleted

**Plan details**

  * Services covered
  * Start date and end date

**Payment details**

  * Payment option
  * Upfront amount paid
  * Payment method
  * Payment status

**Other information**

  * Date and user who created the plan
  * Currency
  * Region

### Service breakdown​

Below the main info card, a table shows usage broken down by each service:

Column| What it shows| **Service**|  Service name| **Package option**|  The pricing package selected| **Price per unit**|  Cost per billable unit| **Unit**|  Billing unit (e.g., hour, token)| **Usage volume**|  Total units consumed| **Spent amount**|  Cost incurred for this service| **% of total**|  This service's share of total plan spend  
---|---  
  
The last row summarizes totals across all services.

* * *

## Renew a Billing Plan​

On the plan detail page, click **Renew billing plan** in the top-right corner. Follow the renewal flow to extend the plan period.

note

Billing plan credits are consumed before Voucher credits and top-up credits. See Billing for the consumption order.
