# Credit History

The Credit History page shows a full transaction log of how your Organization Wallet balance has been consumed or credited — across all Workspaces, services, and time periods.

## Go to Credit History​

In the left sidebar, click **Billing**. The Credit history table appears in the lower section of the Billing overview page.

* * *

## Filter transactions​

Use the filter bar to narrow results:

Filter| Description| **Search**|  Search by transaction source| **Workspace**|  Filter by a specific Workspace. Appears at the Organization-level view only.| **Service**|  Filter by one or more services| **Date range**|  Set a from and to date  
---|---  
  
* * *

## Export​

Click **Export** to download the filtered transaction list as a CSV file.

* * *

## Understand the transaction table​

Each row represents one credit event:

Column| What it shows| **Date**|  Timestamp of the transaction| **Workspace**|  Workspace that incurred the charge. Appears at the Organization-level view only.| **Service**|  The service associated with the charge| **Source**|  What triggered the change — Top-up, Voucher, Billing Plan, or resource charge| **Description**|  Additional context for the transaction| **Code**|  Voucher code or billing plan code, if applicable| **Plan ID**|  Billing plan identifier, if the charge is plan-related| **Status**|  Success or Expired| **Amount**|  Credits added (positive) or deducted (negative)  
---|---  
  
Use pagination at the bottom of the table to navigate through large transaction sets.

* * *

## What's next​

  * Billing Plans — view plan-level credit allocations and usage.
