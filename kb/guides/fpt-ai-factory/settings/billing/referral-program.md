# Referral Program

The Referral Program is a mutual reward system. Invite friends using your unique referral link to earn credits for yourself, while your friends receive a bonus on their first top-ups.

## Rewards​

Who| Reward| You (the referrer)| $25 credit per successful referral| Your friend (the invitee)| 30% bonus on top-ups, up to $150, for the first 30 days  
---|---  
  
Rewards are applied automatically once your friend signs up and completes a qualifying first top-up of at least $25.

## How to share your link​

  1. Go to the **Referral** page on your dashboard.
  2. Click **Copy** to copy your unique referral link.
  3. Share the link with friends.

## Track your referrals​

Monitor your referral performance in the **Summary** section of the Referral page:

Metric| What it shows| **Total registered users**|  Number of friends who created an account using your link.| **Referral credits**|  Total bonus amount ($) earned from your friends' successful top-ups.  
---|---

## Terms and conditions​

  * **Eligibility** — the Referral Program is available to all registered FPT AI Factory Portal users.
  * **Qualifying top-up** — a referral is only considered successful when the invited user signs up and completes a first top-up of at least $25.
  * **Referrer reward** — $25 credit is added to your Wallet per successful referral. There is no stated limit on the number of referrals.
  * **Invitee reward** — the invited user receives a 30% bonus on all top-ups made within the first 30 days after sign-up, capped at $150 total.
  * **Automatic application** — both rewards are applied automatically. No manual claim is required.
  * **Non-transferable** — referral credits cannot be transferred, withdrawn as cash, or applied to another account.
  * **Abuse prevention** — FPT reserves the right to disqualify referrals that appear fraudulent, self-referred, or in violation of platform terms.
