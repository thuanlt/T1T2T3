# Pricing

The Pricing page shows the current rates for all FPT AI Factory services. Use it to estimate costs before launching resources or to compare pricing across service tiers.

## Go to Pricing​

In the top navigation bar, click **Pricing**. The page opens on the default product (GPU VM).

* * *

## Navigate between services​

A sidebar or tab list on the left shows all available services:

  * **GPU Virtual Machine** — GPU Virtual Machine hourly rates
  * **GPU Container** — Container-based GPU compute rates
  * **Network Volume** — Storage pricing based on provisioned capacity (GB/hour) for high-performance GPU compute workloads
  * **Token Factory** — API-based inference rates (Serverless and Dedicated endpoints)

Click any service name to load its pricing table.

Some services have multiple tabs — for example, **Token Factory** splits into **Serverless endpoint** and **Dedicated endpoint**. Click the tab to switch between pricing tiers.

* * *

## Switch currency​

In the top-right area of the pricing page, use the **currency selector** to switch between **USD** and **JPY**. All prices update immediately.

note

The displayed currency is for reference only. Your actual billing currency is set during your first top-up and cannot be changed afterward. See Billing for details.

* * *

## Read a pricing table​

Each pricing table is organized into sections by model or service component. A typical row shows:

Column| Description| **Model / Service**|  Name of the model or service component| **Billing unit**|  What you are charged per (e.g., hour, 1M tokens, request)| **Input price**|  Cost per unit for input (where applicable)| **Output price**|  Cost per unit for output (where applicable)  
---|---  
  
Some services show different columns depending on the billing model — for example, GPU VM shows a per-hour rate, while AI Inference shows per-token rates for input and output separately.

* * *

## How billing works​

Click **How billing works?** at the top of the pricing page to open a drawer with detailed billing explanations for the selected service.

The drawer covers each billable component and shows:

  * **Minimum billable usage** — the smallest unit you are charged for
  * **Billing frequency** — how often charges are calculated
  * **Billing equation** — the formula used to compute your charge
  * **Example** — a worked calculation with sample values
