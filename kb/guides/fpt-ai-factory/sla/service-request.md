# 4\. SERVICE REQUEST

The service requests are general guidance, technical assistance or information requests that do not cause disruption, do not affect revenue, brand, customer experience, or service quality.

## 4.1 Service request types ​

No.| Type| Description| Example| 1| General| Contact for sales, compliance| Get information about new products, Pricing, Billing, Contract, etc.| 2| Technical| Technical issues while using the service but does not cause disruption.| 

  * Reset the password or unlock the account for the tenancy administrator 
  * Add or change a tenancy administrator 
  * Feature configuration 

| 3| Additional resources| Increment or adjustment services| Request a service limit (quota) increase  
---|---|---|---  
  
## 4.2 Service request response time ​

No.| Channel| Type| First response| Resolution time*| 1|  Ticket | General| 1 hour| 1 business day| 2| Technical| 1 hour| 1 business day| | 3| Additional resources| 1 hour| 5 business days**|   
---|---|---|---|---  
  
_*** The resolution time is measured from the agreement of FPT Smart Cloud and the customer is established.**_

_**** In case the resource is out of stock, the resolution time above will not be applied. You will be informed about estimated resource preparation time before we process your order.**_
