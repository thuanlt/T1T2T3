# Monitor Service Health

## Open Service Health​

In the left sidebar, under **Dedicated Inference** , click **My endpoints** , then click the **Service Health** tab.

note

Service Health is currently under development. Full observability metrics will be available in an upcoming release.

## Current Alternatives​

While Service Health is in development, use these existing tools:

Need| Where to look| Token usage and spend| **My endpoints → Usage & Billing** tab| Endpoint running status| **My endpoints** → STATUS column| Deployment logs| Endpoint detail page (click endpoint name)
